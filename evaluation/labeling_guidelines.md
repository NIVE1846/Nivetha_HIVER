# Labeling Guidelines for Golden Set Human Review

## How to use this file

Open `evaluation/golden_set_for_review.csv` in Excel or Google Sheets.

For each row:
1. Read the `customer_message` column carefully.
2. Look at `suggested_intent` — this was generated by a keyword rule, not a human.
3. Decide the correct intent yourself and write it in `human_intent`.
4. If the message is genuinely ambiguous between two intents, set `ambiguous` to `True`
   and write both candidates in `labeling_notes` (e.g. "could be app_bug or playback_issue").
5. If the suggested label is clearly wrong, write the correct one and note why.

**Do NOT copy suggested_intent into human_intent without reading the message.**
The whole point of this review is to catch cases where the keyword rule was wrong.

---

## Valid intent labels

Use exactly these strings (lowercase, with underscores):

- `playback_issue`
- `app_bug`
- `account_login`
- `premium_billing`
- `download_offline`
- `content_search`
- `general_inquiry`

---

## Intent definitions and boundary cases

### playback_issue
**What it covers:** Music stops, skips, shuffles incorrectly, buffers, freezes during
playback, audio cuts out, repeat/loop not working, crossfade problems.

**Key signal:** The app is open and running, but the *audio itself* is misbehaving.

**Examples:**
- "spotify keeps stopping after every song"
- "shuffle plays the same songs over and over"
- "music buffers constantly on wifi"
- "songs skip to the next track randomly"

**Boundary cases:**
- "app crashes when I press play" → `app_bug` (the app itself failed, not just playback)
- "downloaded songs won't play offline" → `download_offline` (offline-specific)
- "this song is not available" → `content_search` (content missing, not playback broken)
- "songs skip because of shuffle" → `playback_issue` (shuffle is a playback feature)

---

### app_bug
**What it covers:** The Spotify app crashes, freezes, shows error codes, won't open,
shows a blank/black screen, or broke after an OS or Spotify update.

**Key signal:** The app itself is not functioning — before or independent of playback.

**Examples:**
- "app crashes every time I open it"
- "getting error code 4 on startup"
- "app is completely blank after updating to iOS 11"
- "the app won't open since the latest update"

**Boundary cases:**
- "music stops playing" → `playback_issue` (app is open, audio is the problem)
- "can't log in, app shows error" → `account_login` if the error is login-specific
- "error 404 on a link" → `app_bug` (broken link/UI element)
- "new update tomorrow?" → `general_inquiry` (asking about updates, not reporting a bug)

---

### account_login
**What it covers:** Can't log in, password reset failures, Facebook/Google login issues,
account locked or suspended, wrong email on account, duplicate accounts.

**Key signal:** The customer cannot access their account.

**Examples:**
- "I forgot my password and the reset email isn't arriving"
- "Facebook login keeps saying wrong password"
- "my account got locked and I can't get back in"
- "I have two accounts and want to merge them"

**Boundary cases:**
- "I logged in but premium features aren't working" → `premium_billing`
- "app crashes before login screen" → `app_bug`
- "how do I change my username?" → `general_inquiry` (informational, not a login failure)
- "I logged in on a different device and it worked" → could be `account_login` or
  `app_bug` depending on whether the issue is account-specific or device-specific

---

### premium_billing
**What it covers:** Unexpected charges, double billing, free trial ended unexpectedly,
can't cancel subscription, refund requests, premium features not working despite
active subscription, student/family/duo plan issues, payment method problems.

**Key signal:** Money, subscription status, or payment is involved.

**Examples:**
- "I was charged twice this month"
- "my free trial ended but I never agreed to be charged"
- "I cancelled but I'm still being billed"
- "I'm on premium but still getting ads"

**Boundary cases:**
- "I'm on premium and the app crashes" → `app_bug` (billing is not the issue)
- "how much does premium cost?" → `general_inquiry` (informational)
- "I want to cancel" → `premium_billing` (subscription action)
- "premium features not available in my country" → `content_search` or `general_inquiry`
  depending on whether it's about content or account access

---

### download_offline
**What it covers:** Songs or playlists won't download, downloaded songs disappear,
offline mode not working, sync issues between devices, storage limit reached,
downloads stuck or very slow.

**Key signal:** The word "download", "offline", "sync", or "saved songs" combined
with a problem.

**Examples:**
- "my downloaded songs keep disappearing after I restart"
- "offline mode says I have no songs even though I downloaded them"
- "downloads are stuck at 0%"
- "playlist won't sync to my phone"

**Boundary cases:**
- "downloaded songs won't play" → `playback_issue` if the audio is broken,
  `download_offline` if the downloads themselves are missing/corrupted
- "I'm offline and can't browse" → `download_offline`
- "trying to download the app" → `app_bug` (installing the app, not downloading music)

---

### content_search
**What it covers:** Can't find a specific song, album, or artist; content missing from
search results; content available in one region but not another; playlist missing or
deleted; library not showing recently added content; podcast not found.

**Key signal:** The customer is looking for specific content that should exist but
doesn't appear.

**Examples:**
- "I can't find this album on Spotify but it's on Apple Music"
- "my playlist disappeared and I can't find it"
- "this artist isn't available in my country"
- "song history is always empty"

**Boundary cases:**
- "I can't find the settings menu" → `app_bug` or `general_inquiry` (UI navigation)
- "song is not available" when playing → `playback_issue` if it was playing before,
  `content_search` if the content is genuinely missing from the catalogue
- "Spotify isn't available in my country" → `content_search` (service availability)

---

### general_inquiry
**What it covers:** How-to questions, feature requests, general feedback (positive or
negative), questions about device compatibility, questions that don't describe a
specific technical problem.

**Key signal:** No specific broken feature. The customer is asking a question or
giving feedback.

**Examples:**
- "can you add a sleep timer feature?"
- "is Spotify available in India?"
- "love the new update, just wanted to say thanks"
- "how do I find my username?"

**Boundary cases:**
- This is the catch-all. Only use it when no other intent fits.
- "thank you, it's fixed now" → `general_inquiry` (resolution confirmation)
- "I don't understand why there's a download limit" → `download_offline` if they
  are experiencing the limit as a problem, `general_inquiry` if just asking why

## Special cases

### Follow-up messages
Many messages in this dataset are follow-ups in a conversation thread (e.g. "yes that
worked, thanks!" or "still not working"). Label these based on what the underlying
issue was, not the surface text of the follow-up.

If you cannot determine the underlying issue from the message alone, label it
`general_inquiry` and note "follow-up message, context unclear" in `labeling_notes`.

### Very short messages
Messages like "ok", "thanks", "done" are almost always follow-ups. Label as
`general_inquiry` unless the message clearly indicates a specific problem.

### Messages with multiple issues
If a customer mentions two distinct problems (e.g. "app crashes AND I was charged
twice"), pick the primary/most urgent issue. Note the secondary issue in
`labeling_notes`.

---

## Ambiguity

Set `ambiguous = True` when:
- Two intents are genuinely equally plausible from the message alone
- You are not confident in your label
- The message is a follow-up and the context is unclear

When ambiguous, still pick the best single label for `human_intent` and explain
your reasoning in `labeling_notes`.

Ambiguous examples will be excluded from precision/recall calculations or reported
separately.

---

## What NOT to do

- Do not copy `suggested_intent` into `human_intent` without reading the message.
- Do not label based on keywords alone — read the full message.
- Do not leave `human_intent` blank unless you genuinely cannot decide.
- Do not use any label not in the valid list above.
